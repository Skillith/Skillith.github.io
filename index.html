LexSecure

In-Tenant Legal Contract Intelligence, Playbook Redlining & Governance Platform

100% In-Tenant M365 & Azure Zero Data Retention (ZDR) Sites.Selected Graph Scoping

The Business Problem & The Idea

Institutional legal departments face strict regulatory and compliance barriers against multi-tenant third-party SaaS AI. LexSecure solves this by running 100% inside the organization's Microsoft 365 and Azure tenant boundary. It guarantees absolute data sovereignty, zero base-model training, and zero reliance on external cloud vendors.

Zero Data Retention & Ephemeral RAM

Powered exclusively by Microsoft Foundry's GPT-6 Luna under commercial ZDR terms. 32KB duplex memory streaming directly into Azure Document Intelligence—zero document files are ever written to server disks.

Playbook-Driven .docx Redlining

Evaluates contracts against institutional playbook standards. Automatically generates valid native Microsoft Word (.docx) documents with tracked changes, counter-proposals, and jurisdictional overrides.

Backoffice Governance & SharePoint Sync

Cryptographically locks Graph API access strictly to designated legal site collections. Features 5-tier sensitivity watermarking, automated statutory retention countdowns, and instant Emergency Legal Hold overrides.
